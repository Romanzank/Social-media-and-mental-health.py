# Social Media Usage & Mental Health: A Deep Data Dive

## Project Overview
In an era where digital connectivity is at its peak, how does our mental well-being keep up? This project performs a comprehensive, end-to-end data analysis to explore the complex relationship between social media consumption and mental health indicators such as **Anxiety, Depression, ADHD traits, and Loneliness.**

Using **Python** and **Pandas**, I transformed raw survey data into actionable insights, identifying which demographics are most at risk and which usage patterns correlate with mental health challenges.

---

## Key Research Questions
* **The Screen Time Trap:** Does increased daily usage directly correlate with higher anxiety and depression scores?
* **Demographic Vulnerabilities:** Which age groups and genders show the highest susceptibility to digital stress?
* **Platform-Specific Impact:** How do different platforms affect users' self-esteem and concentration levels?
* **The Validation Loop:** What is the link between "Validation Seeking" and overall happiness?

---

## Data Science Stack & Techniques

### **The Tech Stack**
* **Python** (Core Analysis)
* **Pandas & NumPy** (Data Manipulation & Cleaning)
* **Matplotlib & Seaborn** (Advanced Data Visualization)

### **Key Techniques Demonstrated**
* **Data Cleaning:** Handling missing values, normalizing categorical responses, and cleaning messy column headers.
* **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Deep diving into demographic distributions and usage frequencies.
* **Feature Engineering:** Mapping subjective survey scales into numerical values for statistical correlation.
* **Bivariate & Multivariate Analysis:** Using Box Plots, Heatmaps, and Violin Plots to visualize the impact of social media across different groups.

---

## Key Insights & Findings
* **High-Risk Groups:** Identified that younger users (18-24) spend significantly more time on platforms and report higher levels of restlessness.
* **The Concentration Gap:** A strong correlation was found between frequent social media distractions and difficulty in concentrating on daily tasks.
* **Gender Trends:** Analysis revealed distinct patterns in how different genders experience "Social Comparison" and "Validation Seeking."
* **Sleep Impact:** Significant evidence suggests that heavy social media usage is a leading factor in reported sleep issues among respondents.
